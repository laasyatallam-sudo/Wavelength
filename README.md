# Wavelength

## Playable link here: https://laasyatallam-sudo.github.io/Wavelength/

### Inspired by the board game Wavelength, this browser game was made for people to play it without needing the physical board game! This game is commonly played between two players, and they take turns giving clues and guessing where they belong on the wheel/spectrum.

#### Instructions
There are two players- one is the clue giver and one is the guesser. At the beginning of the game, both the clue giver and guesser decide on a category to play on. For example, they can play for how introverted/extroverted someone is, with one endpoint being for the introvert and the other being for the extrovert. The clue giver will be given a secret target (which is a number from 1-100) and they will have to think of a clue that fits their target number. Once they thought of a clue, they will hide the target number and give it to the next player to guess. The player will use the clue that they were given to move the slider to match where on the spectrum it would fall. Based on how close their guess is to the target value, they can earn anywhere from 0 to 4 points!
